**SECURE. CONTAIN. PROTECT.**

The SCP Foundation is a collaborative fictional universe centred around an organisation responsible for containing anomalous objects, entities and phenomena.

Its world combines horror, science fiction, mystery and storytelling.

Some anomalies are harmless. Others represent threats on a scale that ordinary people cannot comprehend.

And some things are better left undiscovered.

## Research Philosophy

The Foundation operates under three fundamental principles.

```text
SECURE
  Identify and protect against anomalous threats.

CONTAIN
  Keep the unknown where it belongs.

PROTECT
  Preserve humanity, regardless of the cost.
```

Kayne's approach to SCP-related projects follows a similar philosophy:

**Explore → Understand → Create → Improve → Repeat.**

The goal isn't simply to recreate something that already exists. It's to understand what makes the SCP universe interesting and bring that experience into new projects.
