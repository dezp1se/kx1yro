## Who I Am

I'm **Kayne**, an SCP enthusiast and modder.

My main interest lies within the SCP universe, particularly the games, environments, entities and systems that bring it to life.

I enjoy exploring existing SCP experiences, understanding how they're built and finding ways to expand upon them through modding.

For me, SCP isn't just another horror genre. It's an incredibly interesting universe with endless opportunities for creativity.

## SCP Modding

My focus is on exploring and modifying SCP-related games and experiences.

I'm particularly interested in the technical and creative side of modding, from understanding game structures to working with environments, entities and gameplay systems.

I enjoy taking existing concepts and experimenting with ways to make them bigger, better and more immersive.

## My Approach

I believe modding is about understanding what already exists and discovering what else can be done with it.

My general workflow:

```text
Explore
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Experiment
   ↓
Modify
   ↓
Test
   ↓
Improve
   ↓
Repeat
```

There's always something else to discover, another system to understand or another idea worth experimenting with.
